# Hearth: a living storybook

## Judging alignment

The competition homepage lists Tech Implementation, Design, Potential Impact, and Quality of the Idea. Its Design criterion asks whether the product experience is complete and coherent, with an intuitive interaction model suited to the target device. The page does not give weights in that section. Source: https://amazonappdev2026.devpost.com/ (checked 13 October 2026).

This pass targets that design criterion, not a larger feature checklist. The same character language works with remote controls, phone-microphone conversation, and quiet visits. Browser screenshots remain design evidence, not official Fire TV simulator footage.

## Signature

- Copper-orange fur stays recognizable in both warm and cold homes.
- A little woodland leaf pin and a distinctive forehead tuft give Pip a repeatable silhouette and personal detail.
- Larger eyes contain their own highlights, so the highlights blink with the eyes rather than floating above a closed eyelid.
- Amber firelight, blue winter light, green foliage and parchment controls support the same authored storybook world.

## Performance grammar

Every gesture has a short anticipation, the main performance, and a soft settling beat. Interruption cancels all three: an accepted conversation turn takes priority immediately. Reduced motion retains the pose and expression without squash, bounce or phase animation.

Listening perks the ears and gives an attentive, curious face. Thinking uses a restrained sideways glance, not a frantic loading animation. Speech uses a bounded audio-energy mouth opening, independent of the facial expression. Quiet presence settles into a tender face. Explicit model expressions take priority while their action runs; this is authored presentation, not a claim that the system reliably diagnoses emotion.

## Boundaries

Do not flash the room, steal focus, autoplay speech on arrival, or turn every utterance into a gesture. A funny response earns a giggle or a grand bow; a difficult conversation earns stillness and attention. Evaluate expression readability and comfort at actual television viewing distance with older adults before claiming usability benefits.
